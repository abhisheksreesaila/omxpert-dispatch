Tune the experts, not the model.

Big open models are now mixtures of experts: each word wakes only a few of thousands of experts. Running them at home is hard, with RAM and GPUs scarce and expensive. Our question goes one level deeper than personalizing an agent: can we learn which experts your work uses, from your own session history, and keep those in fast memory? And as your work changes, keep re-learning them.

The usual way

Tune the whole model or agent to you

Fine-tune, prompt, add memory. The model still needs all its experts in memory, or it waits on the disk.

What we're testing

Tune which experts you keep close

Profile your sessions, put your experts in VRAM and RAM, leave the rest on disk. Then stack tricks others have shared on top, and measure each one.

With that in mind

Our session history, two machines, tricks borrowed from others.

Your own sessions

We run the model over past chat and coding sessions and count which experts fire. Only the counts are kept, never the text. 2,000–5,000 tokens of history were enough.
