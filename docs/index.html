Mixture of experts is here to stay

The best open models are mixtures of experts.

"Dense is basically over."

Thom Wolf, Hugging Face co-founder, as Hugging Face released its MoE training handbook (800k GPU hours of experiments). His post · report

Their size is the problem. Their sparsity is the opening: each token wakes only a few experts, and your work keeps waking the same ones.

The idea

Everyone caches experts. We predict them.

Offloading experts to RAM and SSD isn't new; it gets you a few tokens a second. The new part is treating your expert set as something learned: a classifier trained on your sessions picks the experts before you start.

Where it goes from here.

Onboarding

Profiles from your history

Export past sessions or answer a short guided set, then a fifteen-minute profiling run picks your experts. No cold start.

Quality

An adapter for borrowed experts

Train a small correction for a substituted expert. Keep the 2.85× and win back the 8.3%.

Router

A cheap session classifier

An encoder-only model used as a universal classifier picks the profile before the big model loads.

Two lanes

Fast chat, deep work

A small model chats and plans, then hands the task to the big model with the right experts loaded.
